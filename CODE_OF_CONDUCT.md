# Code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Critique ideas and code without harassment, threats, discriminatory language or personal attacks. Do not disclose private information or post sexual or violent material unrelated to the work.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and communication about project participation. Maintainers may remove content, close conversations or restrict access when necessary to protect participants.

Report concerns privately to [yassine@yaschet.com](mailto:yassine@yaschet.com). Include relevant links and context. Reports are handled with discretion; information is shared only as needed to assess and address the concern.
