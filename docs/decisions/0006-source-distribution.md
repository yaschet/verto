# 0006 — Source distribution

## Context

The repository and its engineering handbook have different permissions for modification and redistribution.

## Decision

Distribute source under PolyForm Noncommercial and the handbook under CC BY-NC-ND. Offer commercial permissions through direct contact and retain third-party license notices.

## Consequences

Readers can evaluate the source under its license. Commercial activity and distribution of adapted handbook editions require separate permission. Third-party rights remain independent.

## Alternatives considered

A permissive source license permits commercial redistribution. A single license for code and prose obscures the handbook adaptation restriction.
